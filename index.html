Advanced: raw commands & log

Commands are 4 bytes: three data bytes and a command byte. On AA, off AB, colour R G B 1E, brightness XX 00 00 2A, mode XX 00 00 2C, speed XX 00 00 03, white XX 00 00 69. If something doesn't respond, try bytes here and watch the log.
